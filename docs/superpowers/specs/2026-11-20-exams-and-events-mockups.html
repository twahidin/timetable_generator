Exams, tests and events: mockups (20 Nov 2026)

Every name is made up. my proposal marks something I filled in that you haven't agreed yet. Nothing here is built.

The idea in one page
The exams committee (or whoever runs the event) my proposal says what (an exam, a common test or an event), when (dates, whole day or part of the day) and for whom (the whole school, some levels or some classes). The app knows from the published timetable which lessons still run at every minute of those days. From that it does the slow part: venues, seats, invigilators and backups.
1
Set it up
  • Exam, common test or event
  • Dates from the school calendar
  • Levels or classes
  • Each day: whole day off, or part of the day
  • The papers, or the event's times
2
The app makes it
  • Checks your paper times: no student in two papers at once
  • Picks venues big enough that no running lesson needs
  • Seats every student
  • Picks invigilators who are free then, shared fairly
3
You check and change
  • Move a paper, a room or an invigilator
  • The checks run again at once
  • Clashes and full rooms shown in red
4
Backups
  • Lessons that stop: no relief needed
  • Lessons whose teacher is busy at the exam: sent to the relief team, or "we'll handle it"
5
Timetable committee approves
  • Only for what shows on the main site
  • Sees what changes there
  • Approve, or send back with a note
  • Every later change to the main site comes back here
6
On the main site, for its dates only
  • Exam timetable, seating plans, roster
  • Each teacher's duties, each student's papers and seats
  • The timetable on those dates shows it
  • Afterwards: the usual timetable again
  • Backups go to the relief team my proposal

What it changes for relief

Class is on the exam or event
That lesson doesn't happen, so no relief is needed. Its teacher is free then and can invigilate.
Example: Ms Nadia's 3B Maths on Mon 5 Oct, 08:20. 3B is sitting English.
Teacher is busy at the exam or event
Invigilating, or going with the classes. Relief's "free then" list leaves them out, so relief never picks someone who is invigilating.
Busy at the exam, but has a lesson that still runs
A Sec 4 class keeps its lessons. Backup needed: sent to the relief team as planned relief (approve, notify, acknowledge as usual), or marked "handled ourselves".

Shared with relief: the school calendar (terms, Odd and Even weeks, holidays), personal teacher codes, and the published timetable on a date.

Exams committee and the timetable committee · a new card on the landing page my proposal
Every special schedule of the year in one list. The exams committee (a list of people with their personal codes, like the relief team) my proposal makes and changes them freely. Only putting one on the main site, or changing what's there, waits for the timetable committee. Each schedule has its own dates, levels and status. They can overlap, so a Sec 2 learning journey can fall in the Sec 4 prelims, and the app checks them against each other.

What needs your approval

Only changes to the main site. Any change that would show there comes back to you, whether it's a paper, a date, a venue, a seat or an invigilator. You see just what changed. The approved version stays on the main site until you approve the new one.

Not the exams committee's own work. Drafts, trying things, seating plans, rosters, printouts and downloads stay inside the exams committee. They don't need you.

One exception: when an invigilator is away, the exams committee's replacement goes on at once. It's listed here under "Done without approval".

Withdraw takes it off the main site at once. Backups already sent are cancelled in relief my proposal.
