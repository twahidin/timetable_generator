Stage 2Who sees this: the timetable committee and the relief team (whoever may save Relief settings)
A new card in Relief settings. Weekly lines say who is in charge and who is on standby on each weekday, from when to when. A "No relief duty" line switches relief off (Friday HBL). A one-day change wins over its weekday. A day with no line works as stage 1: the whole team is told and anyone approves. Anyone on the relief team can still approve and send out on any day.
