3 · What "Needs a nudge" means
It is not about setting work on its own. It lists absences that someone else planned for a teacher (a head planning relief for a colleague on Relief Planning), where that teacher has not acknowledged it yet, and it is now past 15:00 on the school day before the first lesson (the "Remind the absent teacher at" time in Relief settings). It is the same moment the app sends that teacher the Telegram reminder: "Reminder: please acknowledge your relief … and set work." A teacher who asked for relief themselves never shows there.

In Relief settings: how long counts as too long

Mark a teacher who would teach more than h min in a row (lessons, relief, exam duties, with no free period between). Empty: no check.
With 20-minute periods, 3 h 40 min is 11 periods: a run of 11 is fine, 12 or more is marked. You can still approve a marked teacher.
You chose: warn and avoid. A long run becomes a mark, like "prefers not": the AI and the app's own order pick someone else when another teacher is free; the warning shows when nobody else is, or when a stronger rule (Flexi Adjunct Teachers first) wins.
