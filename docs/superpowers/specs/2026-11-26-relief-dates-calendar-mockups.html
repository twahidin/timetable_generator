Pick dates: the calendar

These work. Click a day to tick it, or press on a day and drag to another. Press on a ticked day and drag to take days off. Grey days (past, weekends, holidays) can't be ticked, and a drag goes past them. Dates and holidays are made up; today is Thu 26 Nov.

A · Two months, always side by side

This month and next are always shown. The arrows move both on by one month. A drag can run straight from one month into the next. On a phone, the second month sits under the first. My pick: nothing moves around while you drag.

B · One month; the next one opens when you drag to the end
