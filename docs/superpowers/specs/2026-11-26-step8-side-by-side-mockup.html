Mock-up of step 8 in four panels: three classes, each with both weeks, and the lesson panel. A made-up school with made-up names. Nothing here is saved.
  1. Click 1B's Maths with the red mark (Odd Tue). The fourth panel says what "in two places" is about, and the other lesson is outlined in 1A's panel.
  2. With a lesson chosen, move the pointer along its class's rows. Green is clean, red lands with a problem, blue has another lesson (it asks: swap, or put it there anyway), grey is not lesson time. A lesson in another class that blocks a place gets a red dashed outline.
  3. Click a place, or drag the lesson there. Click another lesson of the same class to swap the two. Undo puts it back.
  4. Full screen fills the window with the four panels. Esc goes back.
  5. The list at the top of a panel changes its class.
