Teacher availability

Times a teacher cannot teach (part-time days…). As today: these are not shown on any timetable.

  • One row holds many teachers and more than one day, so the zone class on Tuesday and Thursday is one row.
  • "Who" can be names, a whole department, or all teachers.
  • Times are clock times, as in Teacher availability today.

  • The box says its own name, time and place.
  • Under the timetable, one line for each period with its full times. On a printout this is where the real end time is easiest to read.
  • The timetable ends at 4.20 pm. A period that runs later stops at the edge, with a broken edge and its real end time marked (to 5.30).
  • It shows on the teacher's page, the teacher's printout and step 8's board for that teacher. Class and student timetables never show it.
