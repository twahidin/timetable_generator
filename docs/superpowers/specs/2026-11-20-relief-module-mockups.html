Overview · version 2
Two modules. Relief requests is where management plans relief for a colleague and teachers ask for their own, set work, and acknowledge or cancel. Relief team is where the team sees what's in, approves the AI's picks and follows up. Everyone signs in with their own personal code my proposal.

Module 1 · Relief requests (management and teachers)
A

Management plans

For a colleague who will be away (a course, an event). Picks the date; the app lists that teacher's lessons. For each lesson, choose from who is free, or leave it for the relief team.

B

Or a teacher asks

For themselves, on the app or Telegram: date, whole day or periods, reason, reporting officer (told), and work for each lesson.

C

Absent teacher responds

When management planned it, the absent teacher signs in to acknowledge and set work for each lesson, or cancels if it's not needed.

D

Lands in "What's in"

The relief team sees every request, who submitted it, whether the absent teacher acknowledged and set work, and anything cancelled.

Module 2 · Relief team
1

AI allocates

Planned first, then Flexi Adjunct Teachers (max 3 a day, subject match, spread), then the lightest week. It writes its reason.

2

Team approves

Approve, or Change to someone else from the ranked list. Nothing is sent before this.

3

Telegram to the relief teacher

With the work set and the two buttons, Acknowledge and Request to change. It shows on the published timetable for that date only.

4

Every 15 minutes

The team gets the list of who has not acknowledged yet.

5

Request to change

The relief teacher says why. The AI tells the team and suggests someone new; the team approves again.

6

Cancelled?

If the absent teacher cancels, the relief teacher is told they're no longer needed, and the load is taken back.

7

Load kept

Every relief adds to the teacher's week and term counts the AI reads next time.

8

Weekly summary

Sent to every relief team member, and open any time in the module.

Relief rules
The AI follows these in order and quotes them in its reasons.
1Use planned relief first.
2Then Flexi Adjunct Teachers: no more than 3 relief periods a day each; match the subject where possible; spread their relief over the week.
3Then other teachers, lightest week first (teaching periods plus relief periods so far this week); if tied, whoever has had less relief this term; spread over the week.
4e.g. Never give relief to the Principal or Vice-Principals.
